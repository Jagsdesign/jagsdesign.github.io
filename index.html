AI Co-Recruiter — Agentic Hiring Platform (MAAX multi-agent architecture) · 2024

  • Design lead from ideation to global launch; owned the interaction model, the agent decision-surface system and the mobile-first direction. Five specialised AI agents cover the full funnel — from an employee exit being detected to a structured interview verdict — automating coordination while every key decision (shortlist, reject, advance, hire) stays an explicit human action.
  • Led user research — 30+ conversations across 12 enterprise accounts (recruiters, hiring managers, TA leaders, candidates, interviewers) through 1:1 interviews, shadowing and journey mapping. Found that delay lived in the waiting between steps, not in decision time, which reframed the product as event-triggered rather than task-driven.
  • Made and defended a mobile-first design direction against desktop-default enterprise HR norms: the candidate journey runs on WhatsApp and a branded micro-site, with recruiter and manager views carrying identical state across phone and web — so a manager can shortlist between meetings.
  • Designed the agent “decision surface” pattern: every agent shows match scores, JD-fit reasoning and flags before acting, then waits for an explicit human tap — the mechanism that made enterprise TA teams comfortable letting an agent act on their behalf.
  • Made AI explainability, human authority, candidate recourse and bias auditability core design requirements from day one, as hiring AI is high-risk under the EU AI Act and audit-regulated under NYC Local Law 144.
  • Result: 40%+ reduction in average time-to-hire, live across 100+ enterprise clients, with materially higher candidate response rates and lower drop-off after moving outreach onto WhatsApp.

How I Think

A repeatable way of working.

The same four moves, whether it's a single flow or a company-wide system.

01 — Understand & Discover

Empathy before a pixel

Stakeholder mapping, user interviews and behavioural research to uncover the real problem beneath the surface request. This step is never optional.

02 — Brainstorm & Solve

Make it visible early

Structured ideation and rapid prototyping — decisions made discussable before they're expensive to change in code. Diverge widely, then converge on what's right.

03 — Test & Validate

Design is a hypothesis

Proved by user behaviour and measurable outcomes — not by opinion, seniority, or the loudest voice in the room. Test, measure, decide.

04 — Scale & Repeat

Systems, not screens

Document, componentise and share so good design multiplies across the organisation. Seek criticism over praise, always.
