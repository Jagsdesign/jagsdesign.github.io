04 / Philosophy

How I Think

01🔭Understand & DiscoverDeep empathy before a single pixel. Stakeholder mapping, user interviews, and behavioural research to uncover the real problem beneath the surface request. This step is never optional.
02⚡Brainstorm & SolveStructured ideation, rapid prototyping. Make decisions visible and discussable before they're expensive to change in code. Diverge widely — then converge on what's genuinely right.
03🎯Test & ValidateDesign is a hypothesis — proved by user behaviour and measurable outcomes, not by opinion, seniority, or the loudest voice in the room. Test, measure, decide.
04🔁Scale & RepeatBuild systems, not one-off screens. Document, componentise, share — so good design multiplies across the organisation. Seek criticism over praise, always.
